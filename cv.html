Research Interests

Axion-like particles (ALPs) and dark matter, probed through astrophysical observables: ALP–photon conversion in the photon ring of M87* (EHT), eV-scale ALP dark matter constraints from M87 galaxy spectroscopy across NFW, Burkert, and spike density profiles, and self-interacting ultralight dark matter signatures in the stochastic gravitational-wave background measurable by pulsar timing arrays (NANOGrav, EPTA, PPTA). The common thread is using observations already in hand to constrain what dark matter, and the strong CP problem's missing piece, might actually be.
